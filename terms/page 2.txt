1. Web Application
It’s basically software that runs straight inside your browser—no downloads or installs needed. 
Think of sites where you actually do things rather than just read, like checking Gmail, watching YouTube, or managing your bank account.

2. JS (JavaScript)
JavaScript is what brings a webpage to life. Without it, a site is just a static digital poster. 
JS is the brain behind the scenes that makes buttons actually work, loads new data without refreshing, and powers React.

3. Function
A function is just a handy chunk of code you write once to do a specific job, and then reuse whenever you need it. 
In React, every single piece of your screen—like a navbar or button—is actually built as a function.

4. CSS
CSS is what makes a plain webpage actually look good. 
It controls colors, fonts, spacing, and how layouts adapt to phones or 
laptops—turning boring black-and-white text into a clean, modern design.
The styling engine

5. UI
UI is everything you see and touch on your screen—buttons, icons, menus, and text boxes. 
A great UI makes an app feel intuitive and inviting so users instantly know where to click.
The Visual screen
